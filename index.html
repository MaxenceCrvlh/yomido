Yomidō 読み道

Kanji, kana and Japanese vocabulary flashcards, writing practice, and a reading tool for real Japanese pages. For iPhone and iPad. Free, with no account and no data collected.

Support

A question, a bug, an idea? Write to mc.app.support@proton.me.

With Settings → iCloud → Sync with iCloud turned on, your progress is kept in your own iCloud account and shared between your iPhone and iPad. You can also save a backup file at any time: Settings → Save backup file, then Restore from file on the other device.
